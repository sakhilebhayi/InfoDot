## Scope
Operate surface: the whole signed-in area (shell, Home, Knowledge, Platforms, Analytics, account and team administration). Audience: business people at small companies through enterprises; job: find a document or answer, open the right Dot platform, administer access.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A calm, precise corporate workspace. Order from type, spacing and alignment; information in tables; one restrained accent. It refuses the dark control-room look of the sibling Dot platforms, social cues (likes, counters, feeds) and decorative colour.
OWN-WORLD: Neutral cool greys, white surfaces, hairline borders, one blue accent from the InfoDot logo (#1b64b4 light, #5ea1f0 dark), amber and green only for state. Hanken Grotesk only, tabular figures, light Material Symbols. 6-8px radii, popover shadows only. Light and dark follow the OS with equal contrast.
STORY: A signed-in person lands on Home, sees what is waiting for an answer and their team, opens Files or Documents signed in from the left navigation, and always knows where they are from the breadcrumb.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 248px side navigation grouped Work, Knowledge, Ecosystem, Administration; 56px top bar with breadcrumbs, search, Dot Apps, team and account; content column with a page title, one line of purpose and a table.
FORM: Standard enterprise app shell (side navigation, breadcrumb top bar, tables). Extension of the incumbent identity by owner brief; seed key: owner-pinned-enterprise.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
