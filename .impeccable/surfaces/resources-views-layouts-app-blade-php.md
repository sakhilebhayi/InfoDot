## Scope
Operate surface: the whole signed-in area. Audience: business owners, teams and administrators at South African companies, small to enterprise. Job: run the company's knowledge and documents, open the right Dot platform, administer people.

## Direction contract
THESIS: InfoDot is where a company's things are owned, so every thing carries its owner. The company's 4-letter owner code (like a container's) and a serial mark everything it owns, and each business area owns a container colour. It refuses the sidebar-plus-header-plus-identical-cards dashboard, the sibling Dot looks (dark control room, Syne/Inter, Plex, amber) and soft rounded SaaS.
OWN-WORLD: Cool concrete ground (#e4e7e8 light, #121618 dark), graphite top bar (#1b2124), container colours as area fields: Knowledge blue #1d4f91, Files oxide #a63a2b, Documents green #2f6a4a, Platforms mustard #c98f12, Analytics graphite #2b3439, People steel #56636b; off-white stencil ink. Big Shoulders Stencil Display for owner codes and big numerals only; Archivo for everything read. Square corners (2-4px), flat colour fields, no shadows except popovers, no gradients.
STORY: A person opens InfoDot and sees their company's code and name first, what is waiting for them in their own words, and doors to Files and Documents that open signed in. Wherever they go, the area colour and the owner code tell them where they are and whose it is.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Graphite top bar: owner code + company name (team menu), area tabs (Home, Knowledge, Files, Documents, Platforms, People), command search, Dot Apps, account. Below it a full-width area colour field with the page title and the reference code. Home: giant stencil owner code, asymmetric area fields (Knowledge large with its waiting questions, Files and Documents as narrow doors), a Next up list where each line carries a reference like NWFN-Q-000014, and the company's people.
FORM: Top-bar shell with area band (no sidebar) and standard tabs, tables and forms. Signature moves: (1) the owner code and reference on everything, searchable (typing a reference opens the item); (2) a stencilled SOLVED stamp when a question is marked solved. Position 5 of 7, seed key 25d79137, assigned and chosen by the owner.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
