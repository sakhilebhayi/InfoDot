This page explains the cookies InfoDot actually sets. There aren't many. InfoDot doesn't run advertising or third-party analytics trackers. Every cookie below is set by InfoDot itself: all but one are strictly necessary to make the platform work. The one that is not (`dot_src`) is only ever set if you allow it in the cookie banner, and you can change that choice at the foot of this page.

## Cookies we set

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `infodot_session` | Keeps you signed in and remembers your session state as you move around InfoDot. Strictly necessary — InfoDot can't function without it. | Ends when your session expires or you sign out. |
| `XSRF-TOKEN` | A security token that protects forms and requests on InfoDot from being forged by another site. Strictly necessary. | Ends when your session expires or you sign out. |
| `dot_consent` | Remembers whether you allowed or declined the optional `dot_src` cookie, so we do not ask again on every page. Strictly necessary. | 1 year, or until you clear it. |
| `dot_src` | Optional, and only with your permission. Set only if you accept it in the cookie banner, and only when you arrived through a link that carries a referral code or a campaign tag, or from a search or social site. Until you choose, the details of that arrival are held only in your visit's session; if you decline, they are discarded. It holds that code or tag, the name of the site you came from (not the address) and the page you landed on, so that if you create an account we can learn which links and channels bring people to InfoDot. It is not shared with anyone, is not used to follow you to other sites and is never set on a plain visit. | 30 days, or until you create an account or clear it. |
| `remember_web_...` | Only set if you tick "Remember me" when signing in. Keeps you signed in on that browser after you close it, so you don't have to log in again next time. | Up to 5 years, or until you sign out or clear it. |

## What we don't use

InfoDot does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics or tracking pixels, or cross-site marketing cookies. The `dot_src` cookie above is first-party, is set only with your permission and is used only to understand how people arrive. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first.

## Cookies set by connected platforms

If you use InfoDot's single sign-on to open a connected Dot Ecosystem platform, that platform may set its own cookies once you land on it — those are covered by that platform's own Cookie Policy, not this one.

## Managing cookies

You can allow or decline the optional `dot_src` cookie below, at any time; declining also removes one that is already set. The other cookies are strictly necessary, so turning them off in your browser will likely break sign-in and core functionality. Most browsers let you view, delete or block cookies through their settings if you'd still like to manage them yourself.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if the cookies InfoDot sets change. We'll change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
