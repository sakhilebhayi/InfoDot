# R-9 acceptance run (section 11 of the owner's brief), 2026-10-28

The brief ([requirements/infodot-community-rewards.md](requirements/infodot-community-rewards.md), section 11) lists twelve things to verify
before the feature is called complete. This is that run: each criterion is matched to the automated tests that cover it (names, not
guesses) and to what could and could not be checked. **What was run today:** InfoDot's full suite (832 passed, 4 skipped, 4,606 assertions,
2026-10-28) and Dot.Dopemine's `tests/Feature/Points` (248 passed, 1,489 assertions). **What was not done:** an end-to-end run on
production with real sign-ins (it needs a person to sign in; see "Owner steps"), and any load measurement.

Legend: **met** = covered by passing tests that assert the criterion; **partly** = some of it covered, the gap is named; **not met** = not built.

## Result

Nine of twelve criteria are met by passing tests (1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9 fully; 6 and 8 in tests only), two are partly met (4, 10, and 12 for accessibility and performance), and one cannot be tested yet (11). **The brief should not be marked complete.** What stands between this and complete:

1. **Collusion and vote-manipulation safeguards (criterion 4)**: a design decision first (what pattern counts as collusion, and what happens: hold for moderator review is the conservative choice that fits the existing approval desk), then a test with a pair of accounts.
2. **Other platforms feeding the ledger (criterion 11, brief section 10)**: choose which platform contributes first; the intake already validates a platform prefix.
3. **Notifications beyond email and expertise beyond topics (criterion 10)**.
4. **A live end-to-end check (criteria 6 and 8)**, below.
5. **Performance and accessibility measurement of the points and moderation screens (criterion 12).**
